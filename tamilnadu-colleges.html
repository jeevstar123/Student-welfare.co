Who Can Apply
Eligibility & Entrance Exams
⚙️
Engineering (B.E / B.Tech)
Via TNEA / JEE Main / JEE Advanced
  • 12th with Physics, Chemistry, Maths (PCM)
  • Minimum 60% aggregate (45% for SC/ST)
  • JEE Main for NITs; JEE Advanced for IITs
  • TNEA for all TN state colleges
  • Age: Must be 17 years by Dec 31, 2025
🏥
Medical (MBBS / BDS / Nursing)
Via NEET UG
  • 12th with Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB)
  • Minimum 50% in PCB (40% for SC/ST)
  • Mandatory NEET UG score
  • TN Medical Counselling (TNDMC)
  • Age: 17–25 years (relaxation for reserved)
💻
Arts & Science (B.Sc / BCA / BBA)
Via Direct / Entrance Test
  • Any stream (Science/Arts/Commerce)
  • Minimum 55–60% in relevant subjects
  • Most colleges: direct merit-based admission
  • Some colleges: written test + interview
  • Apply directly to college websites
⚖️
Law & Management (BA-LLB / BBA)
Via CLAT / Direct Admission
  • Any stream with minimum 45% marks
  • CLAT score for NLU admissions
  • State law college: TN SLAT exam
  • BBA: direct admission, merit basis
  • Age: Max 20 years for BA-LLB

Stay Prepared
Document Checklist for Admission

📄 Academic Documents

  • 12th Mark Sheet (Original + 3 Xerox)
  • 10th Mark Sheet (Original + 3 Xerox)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from School
  • Conduct Certificate from Principal
  • Migration Certificate (if applicable)
  • JEE / NEET / Entrance Score Card
Tip: Get all certificates attested by a Gazetted Officer before submission.

🪪 Identity & Legal Documents

  • Aadhaar Card (Student's own)
  • Community Certificate (BC/MBC/SC/ST)
  • Income Certificate (Parent – current year)
  • Nativity / Residential Certificate
  • Passport-size Photos (12–15 copies)
  • Parent's Aadhaar Card
Tip: Income certificate should be issued by a Tahsildar and not older than 6 months.

💻 Online / Digital Requirements

  • Scanned Photo (JPEG, <100KB)
  • Scanned Signature (JPEG, <50KB)
  • Email ID (Personal / active)
  • Mobile Number (Linked to Aadhaar)
  • Bank Account (Student's name)
  • TNEA / Counselling Registration ID
Tip: Use a professional email ID (e.g., firstname.lastname@gmail.com) for all applications.
Smart Moves
Pro Tips for 12th Students
01
Apply to Multiple Colleges
Don't put all eggs in one basket. Apply to at least 3–5 colleges simultaneously through TNEA and direct quota to maximise your chances.
02
Verify NAAC & NIRF Before Deciding
Check NAAC accreditation grade and NIRF ranking before finalising. Unaccredited colleges may have limited placement opportunities.
03
Visit Campus Before Paying Fees
Physically visit the college. Inspect hostel, lab facilities, internet access, and talk to current students before committing to fee payment.
04
Apply for Scholarships Early
Most TN scholarships open from August. Apply simultaneously with admission. Have income and community certificates ready to avoid last-minute rush.
